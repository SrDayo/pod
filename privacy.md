# Pod privacy policy

Pod is a private, personal AI assistant. It is used only by its owner and is not offered to the public.

What Pod accesses: with the owner's permission, Pod reads and manages the owner's own Gmail, Google Calendar and YouTube data, only to carry out the owner's own requests.

How the data is used: only to help the owner. It is not sold, not shared with other people, and not used for advertising. To write replies, parts of a request may be processed by the AI service that powers Pod (Anthropic's Claude API).

Where it is stored: sign-in tokens and settings are stored only on the owner's own computer. The owner can remove Pod's access at any time in their Google Account (Security, Third-party apps and services).

Google user data: Pod's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Questions: contact the owner through their GitHub profile.
